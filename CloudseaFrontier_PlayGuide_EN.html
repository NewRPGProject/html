■The Early Game

The early game is fairly easy, so you can usually get by without much planning. Progression is almost entirely linear, too.

■After Obtaining Your Ship

You still cannot travel very freely, so follow the story for now. I do recommend making a stop at Frontier Castle, though.

◆Frontier Castle

It is on the island south of the Crimson Hideout. You can exchange Frontier Coins for prizes here.

◆Grand City Byan

An event takes place at the northeastern port. Afterward, speak to Nadel at the guild to begin the operation. Xanad rejoins, followed immediately by a boss battle. You should win without much trouble. A second boss awaits farther in. The boss has powerful magic, and the accompanying soldiers are tough too. However, no reinforcements are summoned, so take them down steadily. A conversation occurs when you reduce the boss's HP to half. Guard against the Art used on the following turn. ↓

◆Crimson Hideout

Speak to Evita to begin a Side Quest. The quest itself does not offer much of a benefit, but it is a prerequisite for other important quests. ↓

■The Seven Star Orbs
(Early Stages)

From here on, you have much more freedom, and story progression becomes more complicated. Main Quest enemies also grow stronger as you progress. Some Side Quests scale slightly as well. Either obtaining one of the Seven Star Orbs or completing two Side Quests triggers an event in the Empire. This event is also required for Azury and Lavelia to join the party. Towns with available Side Quests are marked with a ☆ on the Travel Screen. Keep an eye on those icons and you should not miss any. However, towns you have never visited do not appear. Arena City Midalt, south of Frontier Castle, is particularly easy to miss, so I recommend visiting it early.

■Events in the Empire

As mentioned earlier, meeting the conditions triggers an event in the Empire. You cannot advance the Seven Star Orb events again until you complete it.

◆Ducal City Argus

Azury joins the party. Once you begin the operation, you cannot leave until it is over, so prepare first if you are worried. The situation sounds urgent, but you can take as many detours as you like, apart from advancing the Seven Star Orb events. Amber from the Wild Horse Forest and the Shining Mirror, available for 30 coins, are reassuring to have, but I think you can manage without them. ↓

◆Eldora Castle

Lavelia finally joins permanently after the boss battle. Prepare in the Imperial Capital, then head into the decisive battle. ↓

◆Battlefield

It is time for a major boss battle. The boss's powerful Art deals heavy Thunder damage to the whole party. Keep everyone's HP high enough to stay safe. On the turn after the Art, the boss summons underlings, giving you a little time to heal. ※Be careful: if any underlings remain, the boss attacks normally instead. If you simply cannot win, a party wipe returns you to the Imperial Capital, where you can improve your equipment. After victory, you can fuse level 3 Mage Orbs in the Imperial Capital and various other locations. It is also easy to overlook that Byan Castle is now accessible. There are no particular events there yet, but it contains some treasure. Several Side Quests also become available at this point.

The boss is a very tough opponent. Its Art, Inferno Lance, is especially dangerous and can kill a character outright if you are unprepared. ■Entering the Second Half

Levin travels alone for a short time. This solo section ends quickly, so it is safer not to force battles with ordinary enemies. One character leaves the party for this dungeon only. If you have been using that character, prepare a replacement. Nadel is a safe choice, and there happens to be a bow lying nearby. After a series of battles with ordinary monsters at the exit, Megistus joins. He is your final companion, by the way. If you have been saving stat-boosting items for someone you have yet to meet, feel free to use them now.

◆Rigantz

You can buy Yark Soma, a revival item, here. ↓

◆Realm Gate Northeast of Kaknos

There is a boss battle here. Although the boss only acts once per turn, its Speed is more than twice the usual amount, so it takes turns constantly. It is rather annoying. It is Wind-aligned and weak to Earth, so prepare your Mage Orbs accordingly. Its Defense is fairly low, but its Evasion is high. Physical attacks tend to miss unless you have raised Hit. ■The Seven Star Orbs
(Later Stages)

And so, the search for the Seven Star Orbs enters its second half. As before, Main Quest enemies grow stronger as the story progresses. New Side Quests become available too, but those rated ☆8 or higher are still tough. The "Later Stages" and "Final Stages" sections are mainly grouped by when the events become available. The order you actually complete them in will vary. Apart from the one in the Empire, there are three Realm Gates. They are the Isles Realm Gate, southeast of Frontier Castle; the Cloud River Realm Gate, east of Midalt; and the Snow Realm Gate, in the north of the world map. Using the Stealth Star Orb, available for 20 coins, makes travel more comfortable by letting you avoid battles with weak enemies.

◆The End of One Last Request (Side)

Accept it at the guild in the Imperial Capital. You must have completed One Last Request Returns.

◆Hydra's Avenger (Side)

Difficulty: ☆8. Available in Lake City Serdile. The enemy is a powerful level 44 opponent, so do not push yourself to take it on too early. The boss uses instant-death magic, but Silence works on it just fine. You obtain a material for Azury's ultimate weapon.

◆Desert Treasure (Side)

Difficulty: ☆8. Available in Kaknos. Use the map as a guide and search the north side of the rocky hill. You obtain a material for Xanad's ultimate weapon.

◆Dragon Ship Graveyard (Side)

Difficulty: ☆9. Available in Grand City Byan. The enemy is a powerful level 50 opponent, making this very difficult when it first becomes available. You can actually enter the dungeon itself much earlier. You obtain a material for Lavelia's ultimate weapon.

◆Burrowbear Undercastle

The boss's Art is a potentially lethal physical attack. It deals massive damage to its target, with the shock wave also damaging nearby allies. Characters with high HP and Defense can survive with Guard and some additional protection. Others cannot. The usual approach is to protect them with the Paladin's Techs and Art, or the Astrologer's Gravity Focus. You can also win by pressing the attack and repeatedly reviving fallen allies. ↓

■The Seven Star Orbs
(Final Stages)

If you have been exploring thoroughly, you should be close to exchanging 80 coins for Mystic Ward, which grants immunity to all status ailments. It is extremely reassuring to have for what lies ahead.

◆Cloudsea Battlefield

Return to the Imperial Capital after obtaining your fifth Seven Star Orb to trigger an event. You cannot advance the next Seven Star Orb event until you complete it. The boss is Ice-aligned and weak to Fire. Its physical attacks and breath attacks make it a dangerous opponent. Its Art is a breath attack, so having access to the Hunter's Feather Robe makes the battle safer. Completing this event unlocks several Side Quests.

◆Return to Prison Island (Side)

Difficulty: ☆9. Accept it at the guild in Grand City Byan. You obtain a material for Levin's ultimate weapon.

◆Slar Infestation (Side)

This is a quest at the highest difficulty. It becomes available after the events at the Demon King's Castle, but it is safer to wait until you have gathered all the Seven Star Orbs. Head northwest from Wolf's Workshop and gather information in Mudoo Town. The boss has complicated elemental affinities, so check them with Search Light from Light Magic 1. It also switches between physical and magical modes. It uses an Art on its second turn after hardening, so prepare a defense such as Icy Wall. You obtain a material for Hano's ultimate weapon.

◆The Colossal Request (Side)

This is a quest at the highest difficulty. You can accept it at Mopper Beach. Again, it is safer to wait until you have gathered all the Seven Star Orbs. The strongest armor is in a Safe Room along the way. Do not miss it! The boss has an attack that instantly kills sleeping targets. Always cure Sleep immediately. You obtain a material for Nadel's ultimate weapon.

■The Final Dungeon

Once you have gathered all the Seven Star Orbs, it is time to enter the final dungeon. Ideally, you should complete the Side Quests and have Wolf's Workshop craft ultimate weapons for all four active party members first. Cross the cloudsea to reach the temple area. From there, you must enter the four domains of Fire, Ice, Wind, and Dark and defeat their bosses. None of the maps are particularly complicated, but take care in the Dark Domain. Going the wrong way here sends you into an endless loop. The passage in the third area loops vertically, but the wall beside the candle stand conceals a hidden passage. There are two hidden passages: one leads to the boss's room, and the other to a treasure room. The treasure is the strongest clothing, so do not miss it. The strongest boots are in the Wind Domain. Each boss uses an advanced class's Red Art. These are extremely dangerous, so protect your party as much as possible with Icy Wall or Aero Curtain. Defeating all four bosses opens the way farther in.

◆Final Boss

As you would expect, the final boss is the strongest boss in the game. Around level 65 should make the battle fairly reliable. You can win at level 58 or so, but it takes luck, skill, and a good understanding of the boss's attack patterns. If you have money or Magic Stones left over, strengthen your party at Wolf's Workshop and shops around the world. Buying stat-boosting items from the Secret Shop, southwest of Greattree Costad, is another option.

●Preparation

First of all, Speed matters. If a character's Speed is too low, they may not get a chance to Guard against a powerful attack even after the boss telegraphs it. Characters with high HP may survive anyway, but improve Speed with boots and Accessories wherever possible. Next, prepare for status ailments. Mystic Ward, available for 80 coins, is essential. Give its wearer ways to cure ailments in the rest of the party. A Messiah is ideal; a Hunter with Blue Yark is the next best choice. If neither is possible, at least give the wearer access to Elixir from Light Magic 4. It is also better to give other party members Blue Yark, Purify from Water Magic, or Cool Down from Ice Magic. Inadequate protection against status ailments turns the fight into a matter of luck. Incidentally, Wolf's Workshop can duplicate Mystic Ward for 6,000 Magic Stones. If you dislike relying on luck, you could use the Conversion Star Orb or other means to save up enough Magic Stones. On Normal difficulty, though, you can win without going that far. Every class has its uses, but the Hunter's Feather Robe is especially reassuring. It halves breath damage, including damage from the final boss's Art, making the fight much more reliable.

●First Form

This form mainly uses magic, so Aero Curtain from Wind Magic 4 greatly reduces the damage. Prepare attacks of various elements and exploit weaknesses. The boss itself is weak to Dark, by the way. The mirages also follow fixed attack patterns. For example, the Dark mirage uses instant-death magic, which may seem luck-based at first. However, it always uses it on its third turn. Focus your attacks to defeat it before then, or use Silence. The Earth and Thunder mirages also use troublesome ailments, so taking them down early makes the fight safer. Defeating the first form fully restores the party's HP and cures status ailments, including Knockout. However, MP and TP are not restored. A long battle is inevitable, so do not exhaust your resources too early.

●Second Form

Its attacks are relentless, and even characters at full HP are always at risk of being knocked out. Every party member must have Yark Soma equipped. It uses three kinds of Arts: a sword Art, a breath Art, and a status ailment Art. The sword Art, which deals physical damage, and the breath Art are telegraphed, so prepare your defenses. However, remember that low Speed can leave a character unable to Guard or do anything else in time. Throughout this battle, consider Guarding preemptively with anyone likely to be overtaken before their next turn. The breath Art is particularly dangerous. Make full use of your own Arts to get through it. Even if one character is knocked out, recovery is still possible. After the status ailment Art, the usual approach is to rebuild your party's defenses and health around the character wearing Mystic Ward. Flam is immune to ailments while her Art is active, so timing it for this attack is another option. Then sit back and enjoy the moving ending.
